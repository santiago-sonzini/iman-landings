# HÁBIL — Exploración de identidad visual

Cuatro propuestas conceptuales para una única marca de soportes de casco, soportes de celular y objetos útiles para el hogar y el taller.

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. Son exploraciones visuales, no archivos vectoriales finales ni diseños listos para grabar o fabricar. Las aplicaciones sobre productos y embalajes son orientativas; no representan modelos de fabricación exactos. No se verificó disponibilidad registral del nombre ni similitudes marcarias.

## Criterios

- Una marca para todo el catálogo.
- Símbolos sencillos y variantes de un solo color.
- Tipografía legible y acento en HÁBIL.
- Evaluación conceptual sobre soportes y packaging.
- Antes de producción: vectorizar el diseño elegido, definir versiones y tamaños mínimos y probar reproducción real.

## Prompts de generación

### 01 — INGENIO INDUSTRIAL

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE polished brand identity concept presentation board for the Argentine brand HÁBIL, selling useful everyday physical products: 3D-printed wall mounted motorcycle helmet supports, desk phone stands, bag clips and practical home/workshop accessories. ONE coherent umbrella brand, not multiple sub-brands.
This is a new creative logo and branding exploration, not a reconstruction of existing products. Mockups are conceptual.
Landscape 3:2 layout, premium professional brand designer presentation, generous whitespace and clear editorial hierarchy. Main original vector-friendly logo and wordmark dominate the top half. Lower portion has a clear small solid-black monochrome logo specimen on white, three color swatches, and two tasteful conceptual applications: a small retail cardboard sleeve around a matte polymer desktop phone stand, and a close-up of a geometric triangular matte black wall-mounted helmet support with a small debossed or contrasting one-color logo on its face. No need to show helmet itself. A subtle recurring graphic motif shows how the identity extends.
Only use these exact Spanish words plus the unique concept title: "HÁBIL", "Objetos que resuelven". Preserve the acute accent on the Á in the uppercase wordmark. If lowercase is requested, spell "hábil" with the acute accent. No fake contact information, no prices, no website, no additional slogans, no watermark or registration symbols.
Logo must have a distinctive simple silhouette, balanced negative space, thick reproducible details suited for single-color small print and potential debossing. Logos themselves are flat, no gradients, no chrome, no 3D bevel; only the application mockups have realistic material/shadows. Avoid stock lightbulbs, brains, gears, generic swoosh/check marks and helmet silhouettes. Create original concepts, not recognizable existing brand logos.
Concept title (verbatim): "01 — INGENIO INDUSTRIAL".
Direction: precise yet approachable industrial ingenuity. Bold uppercase HÁBIL wordmark in custom wide geometric grotesk, not italic. An original geometric H monogram formed by two sturdy interlocking bracket-like pieces, explicitly legible as an H, hinting at fit and support without resembling a power tool brand. Palette warm orange #F36A35, charcoal #202423, warm ivory #F5F1E8. Tiny bracket shapes organize the layout. Ivory board background, strong orange brand panel. Functional, confident, broadly useful, not motorsport.
```
